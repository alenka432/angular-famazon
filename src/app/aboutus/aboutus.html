Our History - from Garage to Global

Famazon started with a simple idea: Make online shopping easier, faster, and more personal. In 2018, a small team of dreamers, tech geeks, and coffee-fueled entrepreneurs came together in a modest garage with one mission — to build a platform where people could find everything they needed in one place, without the frustration of endless tabs and confusing checkout pages. We launched with just a few hundred products and a lot of ambition. We packed boxes ourselves. We answered customer emails one by one. And we listened — closely — to every review, request, and complaint. It wasn’t always easy, but it was always worth it. As word spread and our community grew, so did we.

By 2020, Famazon had grown into a full-fledged marketplace with thousands of sellers and millions of products — from tech gadgets and fashion to everyday essentials and one-of-a-kind finds. We focused on building smart tools for sellers, fast shipping for buyers, and a platform that felt human, not corporate. Today, Famazon is proud to serve millions of users worldwide, while staying true to the values we started with: Simplicity. Trust. And a deep obsession with customer happiness.

And this is just the beginning...
